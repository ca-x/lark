## Design Context

### Users

Lark listeners browse private collections on a NAS or home server, pick records on a phone and manage albums, metadata and music sources at a desk. A shared link also serves listeners who have never used the library.

### Brand Personality

Calm, tactile and precise. Give the artwork space and make the song, artist and playback state easy to recognize. Use short, specific labels in Chinese and English.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing library, user-selected fonts and theme palettes as the reference for MyGo and Songloft. Show real artwork when available. Preserve each player’s materials and the desktop shell’s system window, menus and shortcuts.

### Design Principles

- Make artwork and song titles playback targets. Keep favorite, playlist and management actions independently accessible.
- Align library rows to shared cover, title and action columns. Give names the flexible space and keep time and controls stable as titles change.
- Use surface tones for sections, a light contact shadow for artwork and stronger separation for floating panels. Give pointer presses immediate feedback and respect reduced motion.
- Keep keyboard focus visible, return it when a panel closes and reserve room for touch controls, safe areas, long names and larger type.
